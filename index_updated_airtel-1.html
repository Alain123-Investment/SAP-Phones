Ibyerekeye SAP MOBILE

SAP MOBILE

SAP MOBILE ni urubuga rwibanda ku rwego rw’ikoranabuhanga n’isoko rya telefoni, rukamamaza kandi rugashyigikira kumenyekanisha brands zikomeye za telefoni nka Apple, Google na Samsung, ndetse n’izindi products zijyanye n’ikoranabuhanga.

SAP MOBILE igamije guhuza abakoresha n’amahirwe ari mu iterambere ry’isoko rya telefoni n’ikoranabuhanga. Urubuga rushobora kandi gutanga uburyo bwo kugura imigabane (shares) mu mishinga cyangwa gahunda zitandukanye, aho umukoresha ashobora kubona inyungu hashingiwe ku mategeko n’imiterere ya gahunda yahisemo.

Abitabira gahunda z’imigabane bashobora gukurikirana inyungu zabo kuri konti yabo, kandi aho gahunda ibiteganya, bakaba bashobora kubona inyungu buri munsi (nyuma yo gukora Tasks/videos) no gusaba kubikuza hakurikijwe amabwiriza agenga urubuga.

SAP MOBILE ifite icyerekezo cyo gukorera ku rwego mpuzamahanga no guteza imbere serivisi zayo, harimo no kugera ku bakoresha bo mu Rwanda ndetse no mu bindi bihugu.
