🏢 Ibyerekeye SAP Phones

SAP Phones ni iki?

SAP Phones ni urubuga rw'ishoramari rushingiye ku migabane ya telefone. Dukorana n'ibigo byamamaye bya Apple, Samsung na Google Pixel mu kumenyekanisha brand zabo binyuze mu migabane igurishwa ku bakoresha.

Apple

Apple izwi ku iPhone, iPad na Mac. Imigabane yacu ishingiye ku bikoresho byabo.

Samsung

Samsung ikora Galaxy n'ibindi bikoresho. Dufite imigabane ishingiye kuri brand yabo.

Google Pixel

Pixel ni telefone za Google. Na zo ziri mu migabane yacu.
